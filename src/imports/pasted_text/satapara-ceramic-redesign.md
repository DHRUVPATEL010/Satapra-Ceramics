# Figma Redesign Prompt – Satapara Ceramic Trading Company

Redesign the existing Figma website while preserving its premium, modern, luxury aesthetic. The current design quality is excellent—do **not** simplify it. Instead, transform it into a website that clearly represents **Satapara Ceramic**, a **ceramic tiles and sanitaryware trading company** rather than a manufacturer.

## Design Direction

Keep the website looking like an **Awwwards-level premium website**, but shift the visual identity from a luxury brand showcase to a **professional ceramic trading company**.

The website should communicate:

* Large product variety
* Trusted trading partner
* Multiple premium brands
* Expert product consultation
* Wholesale & Retail supply
* Fast availability
* Competitive pricing
* Reliable service

Avoid making the website feel like a manufacturing company.
